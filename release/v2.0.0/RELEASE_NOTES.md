
# Version 2.0.0 release notes

Version 2.0.0 is the reproducibility release supporting
*Receiver Provenance, Population Selection, and Reliability Trade-offs in RSSI Localization*.

Exact Zenodo DOI: `10.5281/zenodo.23083321`  
Concept DOI: `10.5281/zenodo.22143329`  
Previous exact release: `10.5281/zenodo.22703158`

## Added since v1.0.0

- post-rejection objective and fitting-validation evidence;
- representation-preserving and matched-population analyses;
- fixed-candidate map-selection evidence;
- exact-correspondence portability and refusal tests;
- preservation of a separately authored preparation workflow;
- native reliability/calibration and source-expression analyses;
- DSI Wi-Fi transfer and chronological LoRaWAN analyses;
- post-hoc equal-acceptance analysis;
- dependency/interface verification;
- public source/provenance documentation and verification receipts.

Favorable and adverse results are retained.

This release does not claim a universally superior localizer, a new general
conformal method, physical authentication of gateway coordinates, or universal
transfer of reliability relationships.

Third-party raw measurements and externally authored source material remain
under their respective source terms and are obtained separately where required.
