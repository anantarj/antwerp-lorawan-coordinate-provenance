The original DAE notebook, DAE_script.py and geographic helper were supplied by the user from the versioned Anagnostopoulos–Kalousis DAE code deposit, DOI 10.5281/zenodo.5589651. They remain clearly identified under `sources/original/`; the tab-expanded helper files and extracted cells are derivatives for source inspection/execution and do not transfer authorship. All original terms continue to apply. This checkpoint makes no new blanket licensing claim over third-party files.

The G1 core/sidecar snapshots and original row-identity sidecars retain their prior Paper A provenance and hashes. They are not new G2 matching algorithms.

No original `files.zip`, raw radio payload or copy of the full G1 archive is bundled here. The source archive is required separately for native reexecution. Saved prediction/error records are the present study's derived evidence.

Conventional calibration: A. N. Angelopoulos and S. Bates, A Gentle Introduction to Conformal Prediction and Distribution-Free Uncertainty Quantification, arXiv:2107.07511. The rank theorem is used only under its assumptions; this study's within-survey results remain empirical.
